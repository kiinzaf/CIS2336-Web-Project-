About the Student Center

The UH Sugar Land Student Center is your spot to eat, relax, study, and hang out with friends between classes. We built it to bring students together, whether you're grabbing lunch, playing a game, or meeting up with your club.

Hours: Mon-Fri 7 AM-10 PM, Sat-Sun 10 AM-6 PM

Location: 14000 University Blvd, Sugar Land, TX 77479

What the student center offers

Restaurants, a game room with pool, bowling and video games, study rooms you can reserve, and events every week.

Contact us at sugarlandstudentcenter@cougarnet.uh.edu
